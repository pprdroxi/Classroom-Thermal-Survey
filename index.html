Part I

Purpose of the Survey

School Safety Framework Assessment

This survey aims to assess the thermal conditions of classrooms and other school facilities, identify factors contributing to excessive heat exposure, determine its effects on learners and school personnel, and identify appropriate interventions to ensure a safe, comfortable, and conducive learning environment. The results shall serve as a basis for risk assessment, resource allocation, infrastructure investment, and policy development under the School Safety Framework.
